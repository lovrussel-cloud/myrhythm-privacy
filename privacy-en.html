My Rhythm privacy policy

Version prepared on November 25, 2026. App developer: Sergey Seliavko.

Data on your device

My Rhythm stores work schedules, templates, vacations, reminders, spoken text, and settings on your device. You can use the app without an account. The app does not send these records to an app developer server. If Android backup or device transfer is enabled, Android may include the records according to your system settings.

Location, weather, and holidays

If you grant approximate location access, the app can use device location while it is open to determine a country and region. You can also use device location to find a place for the weather forecast, or search for a city manually. Android may use its own location and address lookup services.

Weather uses Open-Meteo. Searching sends the city name and interface language. Forecast requests send the coordinates of the selected place, which can come from approximate device location if you choose that option. Forecasts and selected weather places can be cached on the device.

Holiday requests send a country code and year to Nager.Date where applicable. The app uses GOV.UK's public bank-holiday feed for the United Kingdom and selects the applicable region on the device. The app does not send device coordinates or reminder text to these holiday services. The offline workday calendars do not require a request to these services.

These requests use HTTPS. The services receive connection information such as the requester's network address. Open-Meteo states that its free API logs may contain coordinates and that the logs are deleted after 90 days. You can turn off weather and public holiday display and revoke location access in Android settings.

Voice and alarms

For spoken reminders and previews, the app supplies the reminder text to the text-to-speech engine selected in Android and requests an installed offline voice. If the voice is unavailable, a voice alarm can still play its regular tone. Android handles notification, exact alarm, and full-screen alarm permissions.

Retention, deletion, and contact

Local entries remain until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Android manages system backups. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. For privacy questions, contact the developer at lovrussel@gmail.com.

External services: Open-Meteo terms and privacy, Nager.Date, GOV.UK privacy notice.
